## Rebuild of the point-in-time cache (2026-11-26, owner request)

- **Why it was stale.** The cache was written on 2026-11-23 (commit `5baf1007`), when the point-in-time files began
  around 2023. The 2016 backfill of `data/pit/sp500_members.csv.gz` and `data/pit/bars_1d.csv.gz` (commit `f94178ef`,
  2026-11-25, source github.com/fja05680/sp500, 745 members ever in the window, departed names included) never
  refreshed it. `pitu.lists(repo)` reads the cache whenever it exists, so it kept returning the old 34 months.
- **What was done.** `pitu.lists(repo, rebuild=True)`, the module's own rebuild path, with the same rule (the 100
  members with the highest median dollar volume over the 60 trading days before the month). No script was changed.
  Commit `5406c1f8`.
- **Result.** 129 months, 2016-03 to 2026-11, each with exactly 100 names. Ten months in 2016 and twelve in every year
  from 2017 to 2025 are covered, as are eleven in 2026. This is the point-in-time data that covers the 2017-2021 tuning period.
- **Overlap with the old cache.** 31 of the 34 old months are identical. Three differ:
  - 2024-02 and 2024-03 differ by 3 names each. The old build started with too short a volume history (its bars began
    only a few months earlier), so the new lists are the correct ones.
  - 2026-09 swaps TMUS for TJX. They ranked 101st and 100th, 0.3% apart in median dollar volume (850.3 vs 852.6 million
    dollars a day), and the bars refreshed on 2026-11-25 flipped the tie.
- **Effect on other users.** `scripts/library.py` (intraday strategies on the 2-year 5-minute archive) and
  `scripts/breadth.py` (last 60 trading days) read the cache.
  - The 2024 changes fall before or at the very start of the 5-minute archive.
  - The 2026-09 swap touches only September 2026, which is before the forward window (from 2026-11-16) and early in
    breadth's 60-day span. A recomputed breadth reading for a September day can move by one name in 100.
  - No forward-scored result changes. The freeze starts on 2026-12-07, so this is not a change to a frozen file.
