## What it means

- The frozen calendar is right for the whole test.
- The statistics code computes what it says. The lab gate is deliberately harsher than the textbook deflated Sharpe.
- The trade engine has no private bug on the days that use the stored bars.
- Remaining limit: the cross-check covers baseline A only; the library strategies are checked by the nightly replay
  against the ledger, not by an outside engine.
