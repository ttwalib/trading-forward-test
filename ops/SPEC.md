## The goal

AUTONOMY (owner, 2026-11-23; supersedes every earlier goal): Quantavius uses its own judgment and machine learning on real data to pick its own stocks, develop its own winning strategies and run its own book. The rules, the library and the benchmarks are the foundation it builds on and is measured against. Freeze the referee, not the player: the scoring code freezes at the window's start; Quantavius's decisions keep evolving to the end. No frozen holdout.

## What is judged (the scoreboard)

Three headline results, never pooled (`ops/preregistration.md` section 3): **A1** Quantavius's own weekly book against SPY, equal weight and baseline A (the verdict; `judge_check` in the allocator report), **G1** its own stock picks against the random control list (`data/picks/score.json`), **G2** its own approved strategies forward. Foundation readings, deciding nothing: **H1** the cycle-1 formula (`verdict_check`), the simple allocation rules, H3 (picks under the breakout rule). Success is one sentence: A1 PRIMARY PASS (the book against the equal weight of the foundation sleeves only, `judge_check.verdict.primary`); everything else is supporting evidence (preregistration 0b). Picks count only if the server-side witness saw them before 09:30 New York (`data/witness/picks.jsonl`); every change to the player is in `ops/interventions.jsonl`. The weekly book is the median of three independent samples; a PASS is reported as promising, not proof, with the A1 strict result beside it and the number of weekly decisions stated.
