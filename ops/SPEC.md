## What is judged

Three separate hypotheses, never pooled (`ops/preregistration.md`): **H1** the AI's allocation against SPY, equal weight and baseline A (the verdict; `verdict_check` in the allocator report), **H2** the strategy lab, **H3** the agent picks against the random control list. If a simple rule beats both kinds of AI allocation, the AI reviews its strategies and declares a revised approach aimed at the highest return after costs; H1 is still judged on the frozen formula.
